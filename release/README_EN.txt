HELICOVER 1.0 — support helicopter with a smart crew
GTA San Andreas (Android), CLEO mod

WHAT IT IS
Call a support helicopter: it flies in, follows CJ and covers him with four door gunners.
Drop a four-man team on foot, get picked up by rope, fly to the map marker or Grove Street
and get out there by landing or by rope.

FEATURES
- The pilot stays close, circles to keep CJ in view, avoids buildings, breaks away when the
  helicopter takes fire and orbits threats with the door that has more living gunners.
- Door gunners rate every threat (shooting at CJ, hurt the team, sniper, roof, car racing at CJ)
  and spread out: each takes his own target on his side, two focus the most dangerous one, and a
  target that ducks behind cover gets 4 s of suppressive fire.
- Team on foot: BODYGUARD or ASSAULT tactics, low cover under fire, bounding, flanking, a medic,
  vaulting fences, riding in CJ's car, call-outs ("SNIPER - REAR!", "SHOTS AT CJ - LEFT!"),
  an evacuation advice when CJ is badly hurt.
- Fire modes: protect CJ (default), overwatch (armed only), follow my shots, weapons free, cease fire.
- Crew (Grove Street, Army, SWAT), helicopter (Maverick, Police Maverick, Hunter) and crew weapon
  can be changed on the fly.

REQUIREMENTS
- GTA San Andreas for Android with CLEO (CLEO Android).
- Tested on the 64-bit game with AML. Map-marker flight and spotting shooters by their bullet
  traces work on that game build only; on other builds they switch off by themselves and
  everything else keeps working.

INSTALLATION
1. Close the game.
2. Copy all 7 files from the CLEO folder of the archive into the game's CLEO folder.
3. Replace any older Helicover files; keep no duplicates.
4. Start the game.

HOW TO USE
Open the CLEO menu and choose Helicover. The menu shows only what makes sense right now.
No helicopter: CALL HELICOPTER.
CJ on foot: MODE (tap to switch FOLLOW CJ / HOLD POSITION / PATROL AROUND CJ), GUNNERS >,
ROPE PICKUP, DEPLOY TEAM ON FOOT / RECALL TEAM, SETTINGS >, DISMISS HELICOPTER (asks first).
CJ aboard: FLY TO MAP MARKER, FLY TO GROVE STREET, GET OUT HERE, EXIT BY: LANDING / ROPE.
The vehicle exit button also works: the helicopter lands or lowers CJ by rope.

TIPS
- If it cannot land or hover, move to open, level ground.
- The Hunter has no passenger seat: no boarding or evacuation with it.
- Settings last until the game is restarted.
